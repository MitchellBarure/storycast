for me, there was always something within that made me feel out of place.

Like, this isn't my home. It's a temporary place that I'm staying in. And I'd
always think, like, why can't I go back to Zimbabwe and stay there? Because
that's my home.

That's where I'd feel most comfortable. That's not how I felt in South Africa.
So I think that probably sparked something in me that, OK, why can't we stay in

state, bad governance.

Everything is just terrible in terms of supply to water. People can't even get
water running through their taps. And it's just a lot that just goes on. And I
think that's something that is influencing the mission that I will probably
have.

I'm looking towards how we can make Zimbabwe a better place so that all the
governments can actually come back home and not have to leave the country. In
2023, we discovered that my younger sister, Masha Stansles, was swelling up and
blocking her airway. In that moment, I realized we had two options. We could
either go to a public hospital, but that would mean waiting in a long queue and
having no guarantee of actually seeing a doctor and receiving a solution in the
same day.

Our second option would be to go to a private hospital that can be accessed
through a health insurance or paying cash at that moment. And that would mean
that you would pay an excessively large amount just to receive a consultation
and then pay another excessively large amount to get the solution. Then I
thought to myself, as an African individual who may not know about health
insurance or cannot access a private hospital, what would happen in such a dire
situation? And that is where my mission comes in.

My mission is to increase the quality of health care systems in Zimbabwean
communities, as well as to increase the accessibility to health care for all
individuals living in Zimbabwean communities. I hope to develop a model that can
be applied all over Africa and other communities that are facing the same
situation. I know that in order to see the Zimbabwe that I desire to see that is
transformed, I cannot do that by myself. But I am looking forward towards
collaborating with other individuals that have different missions that will aim
towards transforming Zimbabwe and Africa as a whole.
