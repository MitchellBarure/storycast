Building AmaiReady: An App for Expecting Mothers

Hi, this is Mitchell. So I will be presenting my final software prototype, which is AmaiReady. So "Amai" is a Shona word for mother, but it also sounds like "Am I". And that's very intentional, because AmaiReady is a maternal delivery preparation and support system designed for pregnant women living in rural communities in Zimbabwe.

So what exactly is the problem? Many pregnant women struggle to prepare all the supplies that they need for delivery. Some are asked to source maternity kit items themselves. This includes gloves, cotton wool and maternity pads. And additionally, access to clinics may also be affected by distance, cost and limited information.

So mothers may not know what items are still missing, which clinic is the nearest to them, or where to request for help. So why exactly is this a problem? Missing supplies delay delivery preparation. So this means that mothers would only discover missing items close to their delivery date.

Information is often then scattered between clinic visits, paper records, sometimes even verbal instructions. And support administrators may also struggle to track requests consistently. So this then creates stress during a period that is already vulnerable for pregnant women. So this is the problem that AmaiReady aims to solve.

So AmaiReady brings the main preparation tasks into one system. This is where now you will see a mother can register, log in and create her pregnancy profile, make requests, and so forth. And on the other hand, the administrator can log in securely, review the support requests...
