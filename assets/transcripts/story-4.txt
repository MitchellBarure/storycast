A Day Managing Frontend Interns

Three meetings a week. First, joint stand-up. Short. Where you ask everyone, "What have you done?" and, "Here are the next tasks."

Separate meeting with King David, academic. He does his progress report, weekly progress report: tells you what he's worked on, tell him what he should work on next, challenges he faced.

Same with Joshua. You speak to him. You have to keep his hands busy, because he could do more; he has more time. So you tell him, "OK, these are the tasks. I think this will take about two to three days." So, yeah: "What about this task, this task, this task?" And you follow up on him. And yeah, that is essentially it.
